- Junk dim always have low cardinality
- A junk dimension is used to group together several low-cardinality attributes like flags or status indicators into a single dimension table. This avoids having too many small dimensions and keeps the fact table cleaner and more efficient. It's a smart way to manage miscellaneous data that doesn't fit into core dimensions.
